    Two things to know before you start.
    • The model is downloaded once: about 5.9 GB, kept in this browser's storage, and it needs ~8–10 GB of graphics memory while it runs.
    • Other people's prompts run on your machine. They are never stored, but they pass through your browser.
    What else?
    • Up to ~10 GB of extra system RAM is used while the model is loaded.
    • Your browser is speed-tested after loading. Below 10 tokens/s you can still chat, but the swarm will not send you anyone else's work.
    • Nothing else leaves this page: no files, no screen, no other tabs.
